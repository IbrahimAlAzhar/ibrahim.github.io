2026

  1. JCDL
    LimAgents: Limitation Generation of Scientific Article with LLM Agents and RAG
    Ibrahim Al Azher, Zhishuai Guo, and Hamed Alhoori
    In ACM/IEEE Joint Conference on Digital Libraries (JCDL). Dataset: Hugging Face , 2026
    Abs Bib Website

    A training-free multi-agent framework in which specialist, leader, and master agents critique a scientific article using citation retrieval and iterative feedback. Limitation-unit F1 improved from 37.91 to 52.36 on Mistral-7B and from 19.14 to 53.32 on Qwen2.5-3B over zero-shot baselines; feedback gains varied by model.

  2. AI4SciSci
    FutureLens: Longitudinal Topic Patterns in Scholarly Text and Future-Work References
    Ibrahim Al Azher and Hamed Alhoori
    In AI4SciSci Workshop at the ACM/IEEE Joint Conference on Digital Libraries (JCDL), 2026
    Abs

    Analyzes 5,655 ACL papers with NMF/LDA topic models, seed replication, and coverage-aware longitudinal trends of future-work statements. NMF pairwise ARI ranged 0.418–0.743 versus 0.216–0.263 for LDA; comparisons are descriptive.

2025

  1. EMNLP
    BAGELS: Benchmarking the Automated Generation and Extraction of Limitations from Scholarly Text
    Ibrahim Al Azher, Miftahul Jannat Mokarrama, Zhishuai Guo, Sagnik Ray Choudhury, and Hamed Alhoori
    In Findings of the Association for Computational Linguistics: EMNLP 2025. Dataset: Hugging Face , 2025
    Abs DOI arXiv Bib HTML Code

    Authors often underreport limitations and rely on hedging. We present a complete architecture for computational analysis of research limitations: a dataset of limitations extracted from papers and supplemented with external reviews, retrieval-augmented methods to generate limitations, and PointWise — a fine-grained evaluation that matches atomic Limitation Units rather than relying on paragraph-level similarity — together with a meta-evaluation.

  2. eScience
    FutureGen: A RAG-based Approach to Generate the Future Work of Scientific Article

    Abs arXiv Bib

  1. JCDL
    LimTopic: LLM-based Topic Modeling and Text Summarization for Analyzing Scientific Articles’ Limitations
    Ibrahim Al Azher, Venkata Devesh Reddy Seethi, Akhil Pandey Akella, and Hamed Alhoori
    In Proceedings of the 24th ACM/IEEE Joint Conference on Digital Libraries (JCDL), 2024
    Abs DOI arXiv Bib HTML Code

    Combines BERTopic with LLM-generated titles and summaries to organize limitation statements from scientific articles into interpretable topics and concise Topic Summaries. Few-shot GPT-4 achieved 0.617 coherence and 0.588 silhouette in the topic-plus-summary setting, versus 0.601 and 0.577 for BERTopic alone.
