I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at **Northern Illinois University** (expected Aug. 2027), where I work in the [DATALab](https://datalab-niu.github.io/) with Dr. Hamed Alhoori. I build **verifiable AI systems that expand, rather than narrow, scientific understanding.**

My research sits at the intersection of **AI for Science** and **Science of Science**:

- **Evidence-grounded multi-agent reasoning** — specialist LLM agents that retrieve, verify, and synthesize scholarly evidence to critique papers and surface research limitations and future directions ([LimAgents](https://limagents-novagents-live.onrender.com), BAGELS, SciLimAgents).
- **Verifiable post-training** — reinforcement learning with process-level, checkable rewards (SFT, DPO, step-attributed GRPO) instead of holistic LLM-judge scores (CiteSynth).
- **Preserving distinctive insight** — measuring when machine aggregation discards valid but uncommon findings, and designing synthesis that keeps supported minority views.
- **Trustworthy AI-assisted judgment** — prompt-injection attacks on LLM-assisted peer review, provenance-aware defenses, and mechanistic interpretability of evidence-grounded decisions (SciSec).

My work has appeared in **EMNLP Findings**, **ACM/IEEE JCDL**, **IEEE BigData**, **IEEE DSAA**, **IEEE eScience**, **ACL** and **ICSE** workshops. I received the **3MT Best Paper Presentation Award at JCDL 2024** and have received travel grants from NSF, SIGIR, IEEE, and MMLS, as well as Google Cloud research credits.

Before my Ph.D., I worked as a software engineer building ERP and e-commerce systems in Python/Odoo. I received my B.Sc. in Computer Science from Shahjalal University of Science and Technology, Bangladesh.
