**Published:** ACM/IEEE JCDL 2024 · [Paper](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3677389.3702605) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.10658) · [Code](https://github.com/IbrahimAlAzhar/LimTopic)

LimTopic organizes limitation statements from scientific papers into interpretable themes by combining **BERTopic** with **LLM-generated topic titles and Topic Summaries**. Few-shot GPT-4 reached **0.617 coherence / 0.588 silhouette** in the topic-plus-summary setting, versus 0.601 / 0.577 for BERTopic alone.
