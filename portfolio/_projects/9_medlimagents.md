**Status:** ongoing research

A collaborative biomedical-agent prototype combining **literature retrieval, methodological critique, and auditable security-risk analysis**. The related **SocioLimitShield** project designs a three-layer assessment: paper-level claims and methods, citation-grounded PubMed/OpenAlex evidence, and CDC/ACS/HRSA public-health context — studying subgroup generalizability and societal implications, with expert validation planned.
