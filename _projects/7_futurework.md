**Published:** FutureGen (AI4SC @ IEEE eScience 2025, [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.16561)) · FutureLens (AI4SciSci @ JCDL 2026) · FutureScope (IEEE BigData 2025)

- **FutureGen** — retrieval-augmented future-work generation with iterative self-feedback. A two-iteration GPT-4o-mini RAG setup reached **20.87 ROUGE-L vs. 17.69** for one-pass generation.
- **FutureLens** — longitudinal topic patterns of future-work statements across **5,655 ACL papers** with NMF/LDA, seed replication, and coverage-aware trends (NMF pairwise ARI 0.418–0.743 vs. 0.216–0.263 for LDA).
- **FutureScope** — extends the analysis to **~8,000 ACL papers across thirteen years**.

These analyses are descriptive: shifts in corpus coverage or topic assignment can look like shifts in research priorities, so they are reported with that caveat.
